SCOPE
24 new skills, six classes; unlock at Lv.100 and stars 50/100/150/200. Existing eight equipped slots are retained. Two self buffs plus two ally buffs are retained. Camera shake and skill zoom remain disabled.

Four regions: Dawn Forest (star 1), Crystal Valley (50), Sky Citadel (100), Eclipse Realm (150). Final Eclipse boss requires 200 and now owns the ending. Each region has four camps, four regular enemy types, two elites, and one boss. New 12 CC0 model bases: Quaternius animated animals and KayKit skeletons; provenance/license/hash checks are in assets/vendor/star_monsters.

Gear: 24 class weapons, 24 class outfit families; six craft slots per class/tier. As of v0.9.2 the four shared star wing tiers are retired. Six uploaded class wings drop in all four regions (ordinary 0.5%, elite 3%, boss 20%), require Lv.100 and 100 stars, and cannot be crafted. Original Lv.100 class wings remain available. Weapon/armor/helm/boots from the same class and tier grant a four-piece bonus. Star materials and boss cores persist in save flags. Recipes deduct materials and gold atomically. Rewards can be claimed once per milestone, including old saves already past the milestone.

ART AND TIMING
Owner-bound StarSkillSequence clocks synchronize actual damage, casts, carriers and strikes. Telegraph -> launch -> contact -> dissolve; combo follow-ups restart weapon motion before contact. Reusable geometry with 2/4/6 decorative layers by quality, no new dynamic lights. Real damage does not depend on a decorative VFX allocation succeeding. Swordfall ascends 5 m, holds the complete sword arm skyward, and drops 10 copies of the currently equipped sword at 0.24-second spacing with 0.42-second flights. Heartseeker has five fast stabs plus one heavy final stab.
